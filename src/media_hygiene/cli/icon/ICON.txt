The Media Hygiene icon (0194): the shortcut and the right-click entry of `mh` show it.

- media-hygiene.svg: the drawing, used from 24 px up; media-hygiene-16.svg: the 16 px
  variant, drawn on the pixel grid (no inner frame, a smaller broom).
- media-hygiene.ico: 16 (from the 16 px SVG), 24, 32, 48 and 256 px, rendered with resvg
  (the `resvg-py` package) and written by Pillow (`Image.save(..., sizes=...)`).
- Drawn for this project after an idea generated by Google Gemini (2026-10-16); same
  licence as the project (MIT, see LICENSE).
- `mh shortcut` and `mh explorer` copy the .ico next to mh.ps1 with `docker cp`.
