The meerkat: the author's avatar (https://www.avonture.be), which signs the web pages of
media-hygiene, and its poses.

- avatar.webp: 236 x 320 px, cropped to its outline from the author's 585 x 742 px PNG.
- meerkat-*.webp: the poses, drawn by Google Gemini from the avatar on 2026-10-20 (1024 x 1024
  JPEG on a magenta ground), the ground made transparent, cropped and fitted in 400 x 400 px:
  lookout (the first audit), broom (clean), sorting (sort), magnifier (find), phone (import),
  nas (back up), party (everything done), asleep (the page stopped).
- favicon.png: 64 x 64 px, the avatar centred on a transparent square (0278).
- All WebP but favicon.png, transparent background, quality 90.
