# Faces of the documentation demo

These four faces are **generated by StyleGAN**, a neural network: the people they show do not
exist. They come from Wikimedia Commons, where each file is released into the **public domain**
(read on 2026-10-12). They were cropped to the head and reduced to 300 × 348 pixels; nothing
else was changed. Never add a photo of a real person here.

`tests/support/docs/portraits.py` pastes them into some pictures of the demo library, so that
the real face detector of the image finds three people (and one stranger) for the captures of
`people` and of the `person` rule.
