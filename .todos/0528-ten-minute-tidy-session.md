# 0528 — A "10 minutes of tidying" session from the home page

- **Priority**: Medium
- **Batch**: home
- **Depends**: 0527
- **Files**: `src/media_hygiene/review/home_parts.py`, `src/media_hygiene/review/home_app.py`, `src/media_hygiene/services/home.py`, `src/media_hygiene/review/templates/`

## Asked (maintainer, 2026-10-30)

UX brainstorm: a short session makes a 500,000-photo library approachable a little at a time.

## What

- A home tile "10 minutes of tidying": opens the review pages in turn on the cases with the
  biggest gain (0527), a bar counting the time and the gain; at 10 minutes it proposes to stop,
  *Apply now* offered, the summary shown, or to go on.
- 10 minutes: a named constant (few settings rule).
- Everything shown as photos before it is set aside (0524).

## Done when

The session chains at least bursts and near duplicates, stops at the time, and ends on
*Apply now* and one 👉 What next line.
