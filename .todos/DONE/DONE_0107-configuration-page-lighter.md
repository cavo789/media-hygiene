# 0107 — Step 7 (configuration file) is 730 lines: lead with the three settings people change

- **Priority**: Medium — the longest page of the guide sits in the middle of the beginner path
- **Batch**: docs
- **Depends**: —
- **Files**: `documentation/en/clean/07-configuration-file.md`, `documentation/fr/clean/07-configuration-file.md`, `tests/support/docs/scenario.py`

## Context

UX review of 2026-10-12. `clean/07-configuration-file.md` is 730 lines, four times any other
step: its *Create the file* section is followed by the full output of `config` (the table of
every setting, then the whole commented `config.toml`), about 330 lines of captured text,
before *Fill it in* tells the reader what to write. A beginner scrolls through settings named
`classify.ai.*`, `places.*`, `people.*` while they only wanted French and their time zone.

## Goal

- Right after *Create the file*: *The three settings most people change* — `general.locale`,
  `general.timezone`, `folders.preferred` — as a ready TOML block, with the path of the file to
  open in Notepad (`notepad "$HOME\media-hygiene\config\config.toml"`).
- The long captures (the `config` table, the full file) go into `<details>` blocks, kept
  regenerated by `docs_screenshots`.
- *Who wins?* stays, after.

## Acceptance

- The first screen of the page shows how to switch to French and set the time zone; captures
  still regenerated; `test_documentation` passes.
